### The manual, the in-app Help, and the app's messages are rewritten for clarity

The manual, the Help window, and the error and interface text now follow ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — short sentences, simple present, active voice, one meaning per word. Sections that explain *why* the app behaves as it does are deliberately still prose: Simplified Technical English is good at stating a mechanism and poor at carrying an argument. The Theory of Operation is unchanged.

Nothing about audio processing or app behaviour has changed. This release is text only.

*Requires macOS 14.0 or later on Apple Silicon.*
